Interested in having your business featured on KCBY?

Not only does everyone in Coppell High School view KCBY, but also members of the community such as subscribers and parents — reaching approximately 6,000 people a week from our website to our social media platforms.

We use professional state-of-the-art equipment to produce a wide variety of advertisements, public service announcements, corporate videos, and more. Our creative and enthusiastic staff is prepared to tailor your specific media needs.

Special Projects
Local Eatery

San Daniele
Advertisement

This video was produced by KCBY's Special Projects team as an advertisement for a local Italian eatery: San Daniele. This advertisement was featured on KCBY's weekly show. The team worked over the course of roughly three weeks to discuss with the restaurant owners and produce a video that represented all of the key aspects they found necessary to show.

Coppell Staple

J Macklin's
Advertisement

For a Coppell community staple — J Macklin's — KCBY's Special Projects team met with the restaurant owners to discuss the restaurant's history in Coppell and the story they wanted to convey. This video was given to J Macklin's for their marketing use.
